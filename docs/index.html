How it works

Three steps, then just press a key

Type a channel

Enter a name like xqc or paste a Twitch, Kick or YouTube link into the LiveGrab dock and hit Start.

Watch in OBS

Their stream shows up in your scene. LiveGrab quietly keeps the last couple of minutes saved in the background.

Press F9

The last 30 seconds (or 15s, 60s, 2m) save instantly. A progress bar shows exactly how long until it's done.

Features

Built for clipping on the go

Everything lives in one panel inside OBS, so you never leave the stream.

9:16 ready

Save widescreen, vertical only, or both. Pick a blurred background or crop to fill the phone screen.

Settings

Set it once, forget it

  • Auto-start the last channel whenever OBS opens.
  • Low latency mode keeps the feed only a few seconds behind on Twitch and Kick.
  • Offline? No problem. LiveGrab waits and connects the moment they go live.
  • Buffer size from 1 to 10 minutes, so you can grab the long ones too.
Install

One installer does everything

It installs what's missing, sets up an OBS profile with scenes and audio ready to go, and adds the dock.

Get set up in about 2 minutes

  1. Close OBS.
  2. Download and run LiveGrabSetup.exe.
  3. It installs OBS, Python and Streamlink if you don't have them.
  4. Open OBS. It starts on the LiveGrab profile with the dock on the right.
  5. Type a channel, click Start, press F9.
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

FAQ

Questions

Is LiveGrab free?

Yes. Download it from the releases page and use it as much as you like.

Will it mess with my existing OBS setup?

No. It creates its own "LiveGrab" profile and scene collection and backs up your OBS settings first. Your other profiles and scenes aren't touched, and you can switch back from the Profile and Scene Collection menus.

Can I stream my own game while it's clipping someone else?

Yes. The built-in clip buffer records their stream directly, so it works even when their stream isn't on your screen.

Which sites does it work with?

Twitch, Kick and YouTube Live, plus most other sites that Streamlink supports. Just paste the channel link.

How do updates work?

LiveGrab checks this project's GitHub releases when OBS opens. If there's a new version, click Update in the dock's Settings. It downloads in the background and installs the next time you close OBS.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, go to Apps, find "LiveGrab for OBS" and click Uninstall. OBS and your LiveGrab profile are left in place so nothing breaks.

Can I post clips of other people's streams?

It's their content. Get the streamer's permission or follow each platform's clip rules before posting.
