LIVEGRAB FOR OBS - QUICK GUIDE
==================================

1. Open OBS. It opens on the "LiveGrab" profile with the LiveGrab dock
   on the right. (If the dock is hidden: Docks menu > LiveGrab.)
2. Type a channel in the dock: xqc, twitch.tv/name, kick.com/name or
   youtube.com/@name/live. Click Start.
3. When the dot turns red (LIVE), press F9 or click CLIP IT.
   Clips go to Videos\LiveGrab, plus a 9:16 vertical copy for
   TikTok / Shorts / Reels.

HOTKEYS
  F9   Save clip (last 30s of their stream, original quality)
  F10  Save OBS Replay Buffer (your whole OBS screen)
  F8   Start / stop the grab
  Change them in Settings > Hotkeys (search "LiveGrab").

SCENES
  Watching  - their live stream, full screen
  Standby   - "waiting for stream" card

AUDIO
  You hear the grabbed stream through OBS (Monitor and Output).
  Desktop Audio and Mic are added but muted so clips don't get an echo.
  Unmute Mic if you want your commentary in OBS replays.

SWITCHING BACK
  Profile menu and Scene Collection menu > pick your usual ones.

UNINSTALL
  Windows Settings > Apps > LiveGrab for OBS.

Posting clips of someone else's stream is their content. Get their OK or
follow the platform's clip rules first.
